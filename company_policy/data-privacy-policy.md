## Restricted data

- Never place passwords, access tokens, MFA codes, recovery codes, or customer records in prompts, tickets, logs, or screenshots.
- Use only fictional lab data in shared demos and submitted transcripts.
- Redact personal information that is not necessary for troubleshooting.

## Logging

- Tool names, arguments, status, and sanitized results may be logged for evaluation.
- Secrets must remain in `.env` or a secret manager and must never be submitted.
