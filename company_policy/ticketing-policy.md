## Confirmation boundary

- Drafting a ticket does not require approval.
- Creating a ticket changes external state and requires explicit user confirmation in the current conversation.
- The confirmation must cover the summary, priority, and asset ID when an asset is involved.

## Ticket quality

- Include observed evidence and distinguish it from assumptions.
- Do not include passwords, MFA codes, tokens, or unnecessary personal data.
- Use the incident-response policy when choosing priority.
