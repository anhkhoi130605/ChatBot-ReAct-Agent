## Identity verification

- Never ask for a password, MFA code, recovery code, or private security answer.
- Password reset and account unlock require identity verification through the approved support workflow.
- Looking up support-safe account status is read-only and does not require confirmation.

## Access changes

- Role, group, permission, password, MFA, and account-state changes require explicit approval and an auditable ticket.
- If the requester cannot be verified, provide general guidance and escalate without changing the account.
